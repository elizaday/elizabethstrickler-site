About Elizabeth.

Biography for programs and introductions

Elizabeth Strickler is an emerging-technology strategist, educator, speaker, and creative leader who has spent her career exploring what happens when new technologies meet human imagination.

She began by helping establish internet infrastructure at Georgia State University and later joined Mindspring as one of its first forty employees during the early commercial internet. She went on to work at the convergence of computing and cinema, earning degrees in philosophy, computer science, and digital filmmaking.

At Georgia State, Strickler originated the early concept and technology vision for what became the Creative Media Industries Institute, imagining a former bank trust building as a future-facing laboratory for media, technology, and interdisciplinary experimentation. She designed its initial technology architecture and created the core curriculum for its Media Entrepreneurship program. David Cheshier led the substantial administrative and institutional work required to bring the institute into being.

Her work has since spanned immersive media, blockchain, creative entrepreneurship, generative production, and artificial intelligence. Through StudioNow’s embedded operation at Coca-Cola, she advised creative leadership, trained producers, developed AI production methods, and designed an agentic scripting system. She also teaches AI and creativity in the Founderz × Microsoft AI and Innovation certificate program.

Strickler has spoken at TEDxAtlanta, SXSW, and the MENA ICT Forum; produced the Directors Guild of America Atlanta AI Seminar; and founded the Immersive Impact Review, a peer-reviewed journal devoted to immersive media.

Across every phase of her career, her work has returned to the same questions: What is emerging? What will it change? And how can people use it to create something more meaningful?
