A one-day intensive in which participants develop, direct, and screen original short films using generative production tools.

Designed for creative teams, studios, agencies, educators, and leadership groups, the lab combines story development, visual direction, experimentation, and production. Participants leave with completed work and a method they can continue using.

Private labs can be brought to organizations and events. Public editions are held at Georgia State University’s Creative Media Industries Institute in Atlanta.

About Elizabeth.

Biography for programs and introductions

Elizabeth Strickler is an AI media strategist and educator whose work examines how emerging technologies reshape authorship, creative practice, and production.

At Georgia State University, she directs Media Entrepreneurship and Innovation. She helped develop the Creative Media Industries Institute from its earliest planning, designing its technology architecture and creating the core curriculum for its Media Entrepreneurship program.

Her recent work includes advising creative leadership and training producers through StudioNow’s embedded operation at Coca-Cola’s Atlanta headquarters, where she designed an agentic scripting system. Her consulting has also included Trilith Studios and Dallas Austin Distribution.

Strickler has spoken at TEDxAtlanta, SXSW, and the MENA ICT Forum; produced the Directors Guild of America Atlanta AI Seminar; and founded the Immersive Impact Review, a peer-reviewed journal devoted to immersive media.

She began her career building internet infrastructure and holds degrees in philosophy, computer science, and digital filmmaking. Her work returns to one enduring question: how can people use powerful technologies without surrendering authorship, judgment, or meaning?
