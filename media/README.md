# Media for the homepage

Drop files here with these exact names. Each slot on the page stays hidden until its file exists, so you can add them one at a time.

| File | Where it shows | What to put there | Spec |
|---|---|---|---|
| `film.mp4` | Full-width band right after "The product is not proof" | 8 to 15 seconds of your strongest AI film, the shot that looks least like anyone else's | MP4 (H.264), 1280 px wide, no audio, under 4 MB |
| `film.jpg` | Still shown before the film loads | The first frame of that clip | JPG, 1280 px wide, under 200 KB |
| `lab.mp4` | Top of the Generative Storytelling Lab card | Real people in a real room: a screening, or hands on laptops at the lab | MP4 (H.264), 1280 px wide, no audio, under 4 MB |
| `lab.jpg` | Still for the lab clip | First frame of `lab.mp4` | JPG, 1280 px wide, under 200 KB |
| `stage.jpg` | Above the list of talks | You in front of a full room, shot from behind the audience so the crowd is in frame | JPG, 2000 px wide, under 400 KB |

Everything renders in black and white and warms slightly on hover, so color footage is fine.

Lowercase names only. `Film.MP4` will not show up.
